# Interview Study E-19 — First-session setup friction

Participants: 12 recently activated workspace creators

## Synthesis
Nine of twelve participants described the old setup as front-loaded. Seven explicitly asked to finish profile details after reaching the product. Five said they were unsure why role, team size, and workflow preferences were mandatory before seeing a workspace.

## Interpretation
Qualitative evidence supports deferring nonessential profile questions until after users reach their first value moment. This study does not establish a quantitative activation lift.
