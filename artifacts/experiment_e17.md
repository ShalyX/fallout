# Experiment E-17 — Simplified onboarding

Owner: Growth Product  
Window: 2026-09-03 → 2026-09-17  
Population: New workspace creators  
Platforms: Web, iOS, Android

## Hypothesis
Reducing required onboarding questions from 11 to 4 will increase completed activation by helping users reach their first collaborative workspace sooner.

## Results
Control completed activation: 38.2%.  
Variant completed activation: 42.4%.

The simplified onboarding variant increased completed activation from 38.2% to 42.4%, an 11.0% relative lift.

## Recommendation
Proceed to rollout if no material platform-quality issue is discovered in instrumentation review.

## Caveat noted at decision time
Platform-level event completeness had not yet been independently reconciled against raw Android client logs.
